## Why this exists

Fusion Manage's API surface (v1, v2, and v3) is documented across scattered official docs, a Postman collection, and tribal knowledge from years of building PLM add-ins. This site reconciles those sources into one place — and every page is plain markdown, so an AI agent can read it directly from the repo without crawling rendered HTML. See `/llms.txt` and `/llms-full.txt` at the site root for a machine-readable index.

## How much to trust a given page

Unofficial and community-maintained, so every page is explicit about where its
information came from:

- A **`Confirmed live`** callout means that behaviour was exercised against a
  real tenant on the date shown, and the response is quoted as observed.
- Anything else is transcribed from Autodesk's official Postman collection or
  read out of a production client's source, and says so. Treat it as a strong
  starting point rather than ground truth.
- **`Attempted, inconclusive`** callouts record things that were tried and
  didn't resolve, including what was tried — so nobody repeats the dead end.

Several live-confirmed findings here contradict Autodesk's own official
examples — see [API Versions](/concepts/versioning/) for the reconciliation.
